About Me

I’m Nargis Sultana, a computer science and software engineering student at United International University, Dhaka. My programming journey began with simple C programs in my first semester, and I quickly fell in love with the feeling of building something that actually works.

From there I moved into web development: HTML, CSS and JavaScript first, then React, Node.js and databases. I enjoy building user-friendly interfaces and solving real problems with clean, maintainable code. I’m happiest working on frontend and full stack projects where design and logic meet.

Outside coding I love sketching and painting, playing badminton, reading sci-fi and exploring new cafés around Dhaka. I’m curious, a little perfectionist, and always up for learning something new.
